## Index Notes

- "Fix it" has held first place for 24 consecutive months, and its popularity is closely linked to second place.
- "Make no mistakes" and "No bugs" entered the top 10 this month. Analysts describe them as wishes rather than instructions.
- "Why didn't it work?" is the highest-ranked CS50 entry. It's also the only prompt in the top five that asks a question instead of giving an order.
- "Just give me the code" is rising, but the duck is designed to guide rather than hand over answers, so its success rate remains low.
- "Don't hallucinate" is rising, but there is no evidence that it reduces hallucinations.

## Closing Line

> In industry, people tell the AI to fix it. In CS50, you ask it why it broke. Keep asking why. That's the part that makes you the engineer.
